I am an ecologist with broad interests spanning ecophysiology, hydrology, remote sensing, and biological conservation. I enjoy identifying important problems in environmental science and management, developing deep knowledge of the relevant systems, and using rigorous field observations and technological innovations to tackle said problems.

I am currently a member of the Anderegg and Young labs in the Department of Ecology, Evolution, and Marine Biology at UC Santa Barbara. In late 2026, I will graduate and begin a Simons Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship in Plant Biology under the supervision of Dr. Brian Bailey at UC Davis.

To learn more about my research interests, please visit the [Publications]({{ site.baseurl }}/publications/) and [Research]({{ site.baseurl }}/research/) tabs of this website. People interested in building sap flow sensors should check out the [DIY Sensors]({{ site.baseurl }}/diy-sensors/) tab, and those interested in upscaling sap flux data to whole-tree water use rates should check out the [sapscaleR]({{ site.baseurl }}/sapscaleR/) tab. I also provide links to various datasets I've produced in the [Data]({{ site.baseurl }}/data/) tab.

Feel free to reach out to mburnett (at) ucsb (dot) edu with questions or offers of collaboration!
