I am an ecologist with broad interests spanning ecophysiology, hydrology, remote sensing, and biological conservation. I enjoy identifying important problems in environmental science and management, developing deep knowledge of the relevant systems, and using rigorous field observations and technological innovations to tackle said problems.

I recently earned my PhD from the Anderegg and Young labs in the Department of Ecology, Evolution, and Marine Biology at UC Santa Barbara. I am now starting a Simons Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship in Plant Biology under the supervision of Dr. Brian Bailey in the Plant Simulation Laboratory at UC Davis.

To learn more about my research interests, please visit the [Publications]({{ site.baseurl }}/publications/) and [Research]({{ site.baseurl }}/research/) tabs of this website. People interested in building sap flow sensors and data loggers for analog voltage-based sensors (including FloraPulse microtensiometer probes) should check out the [DIY Sensors]({{ site.baseurl }}/diy-sensors/) tab, and those interested in upscaling sap flux data to whole-tree water use rates should check out the [sapscaleR]({{ site.baseurl }}/sapscaleR/) tab. I also provide links to various datasets I've produced in the [Data]({{ site.baseurl }}/data/) tab.

Feel free to reach out to [mikeburnett@ucdavis.edu](mailto:mikeburnett@ucdavis.edu) with questions or offers of collaboration!
